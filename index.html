Frequently asked questions.

Know what to expect before clicking through to a partner.

Can every offer earn me a gift card or cash? ＋

No. Each partner sets its own terms and reward rules. Some cards are educational guides, and no reward is guaranteed.

Does GiftCash charge me to browse? ＋

Browsing this website is free. A third-party offer may have purchase, subscription, or other conditions.

How does GiftCash make money? ＋

GiftCash may earn commissions from qualifying actions through affiliate links. We aim to disclose affiliate relationships clearly.

Are offers available in every country? ＋

No. Check the partner's official page for location, age, device, and account requirements.

Is GiftCash affiliated with Amazon? ＋

GiftCash is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon or other gift card brands unless explicitly stated.
